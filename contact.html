What's on

Directions

  • On foot

    Five minutes from Harrowfield Market Square. Walk north up Bellwood Street and we are on the corner of Fenwick Lane, under the brown awning.

  • By bus or train

    Buses 4 and 11 stop at Fenwick Lane, right outside. Harrowfield Central station is a 12-minute walk.

  • By bike

    There are six bike stands on Fenwick Lane, beside the side door.

  • By car

    Street parking on Bellwood Street is free for 2 hours. The Market Square car park is five minutes away. There is one accessible bay outside the door.

Opening hours

Thursdays we reopen at 6:30 pm for the recording. Free, first come first seated, and the guest stays for a coffee afterwards.

Breakfast until 11:30 am. Brunch and lunch 11:30 am to 3:00 pm. Cakes, pastry and drinks until close.

PITCHING US

Press

For interviews, features or images, choose Press and partnerships and tell us your outlet, your deadline and what you need. We can supply photos of the room, the logo and a short description of the show.

Lata speaks about the podcast in a personal capacity only. She does not comment on behalf of any employer or institution, and requests about her day job will be declined.

Write to us about press

Guests

We look for people who run something built around a mission and have a decision they are willing to talk about honestly. Tell us who they are, what they run, and the origin story or trade-off that is worth an hour.

Please do not send a press kit. Three sentences is enough. Nobody pays to appear, and we do not take pitches for products.

Privacy

WHAT WE DO WITH YOUR DETAILS

This is a plain summary of what this site collects, why, and how long we keep it. We collect as little as we can.

Template for legal review.

Written in plain language for the study build; it has not been checked by a lawyer and must be reviewed against local privacy law before launch.

Last updated 17 Sep 2026

Who we are

Latte with Lata, 27 Bellwood Street, Harrowfield. For anything about your data, email hello@lattewithlata.example or ask at the counter.

When you book a table or reserve a seat

We collect your name, email, phone number, the date, time and size of your party, and any occasion or notes you choose to add. We use them to hold your table, to contact you if something changes, and to recognise you if you book again. We keep booking records for 12 months after the date of the booking, then delete them.

When you send us a message

We collect your name, email, phone number if you give one, the topic and your message. We use them to reply. We keep messages for 12 months after our last reply, then delete them.

When you join the newsletter

We collect your email address, the date you agreed and the page you signed up on. We use it to send one email a week. We keep it until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any email or by asking us.

Marketing

We only send marketing email if you tick the box. Booking without ticking it changes nothing about your booking.

Who sees it

Cafe staff who need it to run bookings and answer messages. We do not sell your details, share them with advertisers or pass them to the host's employer or any other institution.

Cookies

The public site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The staff sign-in to the booking system uses one essential cookie.

Recording nights

Thursday recordings are audio-recorded and sometimes photographed. We say so at the door. Tell a member of staff if you would rather stay out of photos.

Asking us to delete or correct something

Email hello@lattewithlata.example from the address you used, or ask at the counter. Tell us what you want removed: a booking, a message, your newsletter sign-up or everything. We confirm within 2 working days and finish within 30 days. We may need to keep anything the law requires us to keep.
